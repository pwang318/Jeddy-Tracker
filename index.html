Nurture check-in

Scripts & playbook

Quick language for the calls that grow the business.
Ask a producing agent for the next buyer
"I'd love the chance to earn the next two or three buyers that come your way so you can see how we communicate and execute. Can I earn that?"
Use after a smooth closing or a good update call. Tie it to something specific you did on their last deal.
Existing partner: earn 100% of their business
"What's one thing I could do better to make working together easier for you?"
It invites honest feedback. The agent who answers this honestly is the one you'll earn all their business from.
Pre-approved buyer pipeline review (five questions per buyer)
  1. Are they actively looking?
  2. Do they have showings this week?
  3. Have they stalled?
  4. What's stopping them?
  5. What's the next action and who owns it?
"Is there a property that caught your eye but might be a little more than you wanted to spend?"
Resetting a time-drain agent (lots of calls, no business)
"I want to make sure I'm helpful in the way that matters most to you. When you have a buyer ready to go, what's the best way for us to work together so they're pre-approved fast?"
Then move them to a monthly drip (market update, rate watch) and call only when they send a buyer or ask for help.
Agent who keeps sending not-ready buyers
"Thanks for trusting me with them. Most of these buyers are 3–6 months out because of credit. Would it help if I gave you two quick questions to ask up front, so you know who's ready now and who needs a plan?"
Quick pre-screen questions: "Any late payments or collections in the last year?" and "How much have you set aside for down payment and closing?"
Credit nurture check-in (borrower)
"Quick check-in on your plan. Last time the goal was ___. Where are you on that? Once you hit it, we'll re-pull and get you pre-approved."
Keep it under 10 minutes. Set the next date before you hang up. Text the agent a one-line update.
Prospecting a listing agent from your own deal
"We just closed [property] together. Thanks for making that one smooth. I'd love to be a resource for your buyers too. Could we grab coffee for 20 minutes next week?"
Start here

How to use your tracker

About 15 minutes a day. The tracker tells you who to call. You log what happened.
Once a week · Monday morning (5 min)
1

Upload your data

Go to the Uploads tab and upload:

  • Power BI agent export: updates each agent's closes, leads and prequals. Your notes are never overwritten.
  • Encompass pipeline export: your active loans and the agents on them.
  • Velocify CSV (optional): your leads by referring agent.
Every day (10–15 min)
2

Open Today and work the list

Agents to talk to today is ranked for you: best agents and most overdue first. Call or text, then click Log.

3

Log every touch (30 seconds)

Pick the type and outcome, and write one real sentence about what you talked about. Use 🎤 Talk to dictate.

  • Only a real conversation (Talked, Meeting held/set, Lead received, Asked for business) resets an agent's clock. Voicemails don't.
  • Set a Next touch date if you promised a follow-up. The agent comes back on Today that day.
  • Logged the wrong thing? Click Undo on the pop-up, or delete the touch from the agent's History.
4

Update agents on loans closing soon

Loans closing in 21 days shows both agents on each deal. Send a quick status update and it gets a ✅ for the week.

5

Do nurture check-ins in one block

Credit / not-ready borrowers live on the Nurture tab. Each one gets a goal and a check-in date. When they're due they show on Today. Click Check in, note the progress, and it sets the next date for you.

Growing the business
6

Work your Prospects

The Prospects tab is for agents you want to start working with.

  • ⬆ Upload outreach list: any Excel/CSV with names (plus phone, email or brokerage if you have them). People already in your roster are skipped. Changed your mind? Undo import at the bottom of the page.
  • + Add prospect for one agent, or Suggested from your own deals to add agents from your Encompass files in one click.
  • Move each one forward: Prospect → Contacted → Meeting set → Met → First lead → Partner. Logging a call moves them to Contacted automatically. At Partner they move to your Agents tab.
  • Delete one with 🗑, or tick several and use Delete selected / Move to stage. Every delete can be undone.
7

Check Growth once a week

See where your time went. Time drains are agents getting lots of your time with no leads or closes. Click Monthly drip to move them to a light touch and spend that time on prospects and Core partners.

8

Friday: fill in your Scorecard

Conversations and meetings count automatically. Type in your referred leads, apps, pre-approvals and contracts, then click Save week.
